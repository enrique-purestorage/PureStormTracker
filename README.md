# PureStormTracker

This project leverages the capabilities of **Pure Storage FlashArray** and **Pure Fusion** alongside the **OpenWeatherMap API** to monitor hurricane trajectories and weather conditions that could impact data center operations.

## Key Features:

- **Real-Time Weather Monitoring**: Utilizes the OpenWeatherMap API to gather up-to-date weather forecasts, particularly focusing on hurricane paths and intensities.

- **Predictive Analytics**: Employs Pure Fusion for advanced analytics, allowing for predictive insights into how weather conditions might affect data center performance and integrity.

- **Data Center Safety Protocols**: Automatically triggers safety and contingency protocols on Pure Storage FlashArrays when a storm is detected approaching a data center location. This might include data shifting, system backups, or readiness for failover operations.

- **Visualization Dashboard**: Offers a graphical interface that overlays weather data with data center locations, providing at-a-glance status updates on potential threats and current system health.

- **Alert System**: Sends notifications to IT staff about impending weather events, ensuring proactive management and response to safeguard data and infrastructure.

## Purpose:

The primary objective of PureStormTracker is to ensure that data centers remain operational during severe weather events. By combining Pure Storage's reliable and high-performance storage solutions with meteorological data, this project aims to:

- **Minimize downtime** by preparing for and mitigating the effects of hurricanes.
- **Protect critical data** through smart, automated responses to environmental threats.
- **Enhance operational continuity** with predictive maintenance and rapid response capabilities.

## Open Source Engagement:

PureStormTracker is developed with contributions from the community on GitHub, fostering an environment where developers can collaborate to refine algorithms, improve data integration, and enhance the resilience of data centers worldwide against natural disasters.
